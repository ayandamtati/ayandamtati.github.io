My name is Ayanda Mtati. I am interested in understanding how people, processes, and organisations work together — and how better use of evidence can lead to better decisions.

My academic background in Industrial Psychology has shaped my interest in people at work, while my growing technical capabilities allow me to approach organisational and operational questions through data, analysis, and structured problem-solving.

### What I focus on

My interests sit at the intersection of people and operations.

I am particularly interested in:
 - 👥 Employee experience, wellbeing, retention, and organisational effectiveness
 - ⚙️ Operational performance and process improvement
 - 📊 Data analysis and decision support
 - 🌱 ID&E and workplace belonging
 - 🎓 Youth and graduate employment and job readiness
 - 🔎 Research into how organisational problems can be better understood through evidence

### How I approach problems

I enjoy taking something that is difficult to understand and making it more structured.

That might mean analysing operational data, investigating why employees leave, improving access to organisational information, or designing a way to turn evidence into something that supports better decisions.

My approach is simple:

Understand → Analyse → Improve → Communicate

### What you'll find here

This site brings together my professional development, projects, research interests, and experiments.

Some work is practical and operations-focused. Other work explores organisational questions through research, analytical experiments, and prototypes.

The common thread is a curiosity about how organisations can work better — for both the people within them and the systems they depend on.
